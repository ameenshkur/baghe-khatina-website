The company

From Baghdad,
across Iraq.

We connect international brands with Iraqi customers through representation, distribution and beauty retail.

Baghe Khatina began in Baghdad as a commercial agency for cosmetics and accessories. Our relationship with Flormar helped shape a business spanning product distribution and stores under both the Flormar and Baghe Khatina names.

Today, our portfolio brings together makeup, skincare, haircare and fragrance. We link brand relationships with wholesale distribution and the store experience, helping products reach the Iraqi market.
