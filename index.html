Explore the company

Who we are

A local partner
for global beauty.

Baghe Khatina brings international beauty brands into the Iraqi market through one connected business: representation, distribution and retail.

Founded in Baghdad as a commercial agency for cosmetics and accessories, the company grew through its relationship with Flormar into distribution and stores under both names.

Our portfolio now spans makeup, skincare, haircare and fragrance. We work across the steps that take a brand from a commercial relationship to a product customers can find in store.

What we do

From a brand’s first conversation in Iraq to its place on a shelf, our work connects the commercial steps.
