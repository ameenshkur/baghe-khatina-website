The company

A beauty business,
rooted in Baghdad.

Baghe Khatina connects international beauty with the Iraqi market.

Established in Baghdad as a commercial agency for cosmetics and accessories, the company grew through its work with Flormar, distribution across Iraqi governorates and an expanding retail presence.

Its story brings together three areas of experience: building brand relationships, understanding local markets and creating places where customers can discover beauty.
