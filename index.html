Our story

Built in Baghdad. Growing across the country.

Baghe Khatina was founded in Baghdad as a commercial agency for beauty and accessories. The company profile describes its introduction of Flormar to Iraq, followed by distribution across the governorates and a Flormar branch at Family Mall in Erbil.

Its business grew beyond wholesale into a retail network under the Flormar and Baghe Khatina names. The company also developed its own cosmetics and accessories lines.
